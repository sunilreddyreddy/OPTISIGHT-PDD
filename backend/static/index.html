Eye Wellness & Preventative Care

Scientifically validated ergonomics and visual endurance practices
20:00 Focus Interval

The 20-20-20 Rest Rule

To prevent Digital Eye Strain (Asthenopia), take a 20-second break every 20 minutes to gaze at something 20 feet (6 meters) away.

Ergonomic Screen Positioning

Position computer displays 20 to 24 inches away with the top of the monitor level with eye height so you gaze slightly downward.

Active Blinking & Hydration

Digital screens reduce natural blink rates by 50%. Practice deliberate blinking and stay well hydrated to protect the tear film.

Ambient Lighting Balance

Avoid high contrast glare. Ensure room lighting matches the brightness of your display and eliminate harsh backlights.

Ocular Nutrition (Lutein & Zeaxanthin)

Nutrients such as Lutein, Zeaxanthin, Omega-3 fatty acids, and Vitamin A promote retinal and macular cell vitality.
